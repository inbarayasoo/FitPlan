FitPlan

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

FitPlan is a small workout-tracking application. This page explains what personal data it stores and why. If anything here is unclear, contact the address at the bottom.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, a display name, and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash (Argon2id); it is never stored or shown in plain text.
  • Training data you enter — exercises, workout plans, logged sessions and sets, and per-exercise notes.
  • If you use "Sign in with Google" — we receive the email address and name on your Google account. We do not receive your Google password and request no other Google data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session (a signed token, no server-side session store).
  • To show a coach the trainees on their roster and those trainees' training data.
  • To confirm your email address: when you register, a six-digit code is sent to your address through Brevo, a transactional-email provider. Only your address and the code are sent.

Where it is stored

In a SQLite database on a server hosted by Fly.io (Frankfurt region). The connection to the site is encrypted (HTTPS).

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties, other than the email provider named above, which only handles the verification message. Within the app, a coach can see the training data of trainees on their own roster.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To have your account and its data deleted, email the address below.
